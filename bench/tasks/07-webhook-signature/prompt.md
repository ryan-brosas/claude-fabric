hookrelay forwards inbound partner webhooks straight to the queue, without checking who sent them. We agreed on an `x-relay-signature: t=<unix seconds>,v1=<hex>` header where the hex is the HMAC-SHA256 of `<t>.<raw body>` with our secret, and a request is stale when `t` is more than `toleranceSeconds` away from our clock in either direction. Please verify the header before anything is enqueued: 400 with `{ "error": "malformed_signature" }` when we cannot parse it, 401 with `{ "error": "timestamp_out_of_range" }` for a stale timestamp, 401 with `{ "error": "bad_signature" }` for a wrong signature, and keep the 202 for a good one. Put the check in `src/webhook/verify.ts` so it can be tested on its own, and make sure a rejected request never reaches the queue.
